Stormwind Stockade

You can find the Stormwind Stockade dungeon within Stormwind City, Eastern Kingdoms.
The coordinates are (51.0,67.2)

Click for Dungeon Entrance:

Route:

Map 1

Bosses:

1. Lord Overheat2. Randolph Moloch3. Hogger
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Deals fire damage spells. Casts fireballs around the room.
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Vanishes then reappears. Deals physical damage attacks, stabs current target.
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Slices target dealing physical damage. Enrages at 30% increasing his speed & damage done, nuke boss. Interrupt Maddening Call.
