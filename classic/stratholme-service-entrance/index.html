Stratholme: Service Entrance

You can find the Stratholme Service dungeon entrance within Eastern Plaguelands, Eastern Kingdoms.
The coordinates are (43.3,19.4)

Click for Dungeon Entrance:

Route:

Map 1

Bosses:

1. Baroness Anastari2. Nerub'enkan3. Maleki the Pallid4. Magistrate Barthilas5. Ramstein the Gorger6. Lord Aurius Rivendare
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Silences all nearby for 6 sec. Curses all nearby for 12 sec. Banshee Wail inflicts shadow damage to current target. Possess controls a player, reduce their health to 50% to free them.
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Encasing Webs roots player. Undead Scarabs poison players. Crypt Scarabs sends scarabs to attack bosses current target.
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Uses Frost Bolt. Ice Tomb freezes current target, this stuns. Drain Life inflicts shadow damage & heals boss. During Frost Armor use spell casting as melee is slowed.
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Enrages increasing damage & speed, this stacks. Inflicts physical damage & knock backs. Draining Blow inflicts shadow damage & heals boss.
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Knocks back current target & stuns. All nearby players take physical damage during Trample.
Quick Boss Tactics:
/i Deals physical & shadow damage to players. Aoe the Mindless Skeletons. Heals himself with any remaining Mindless Skeletons after 12 sec.
