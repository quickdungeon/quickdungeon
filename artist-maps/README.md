# Maps by other artists (originals, not deployed)

Unmodified copies of third-party maps used on the site, kept so there's a record of exactly
what each artist published. The site uses the copies in `site/content/dungeons/<slug>/images/`.

## Santiago Reyes: Atlas de Azeroth: Forever (https://asguevara433.wixsite.com/srmaps)

Terms (from his project post): free to use; don't alter the watermark or credits; no use for
profit; he appreciates being asked to approve uses (https://asguevara433.wixsite.com/srmaps/contacto).
Every page that shows one of these maps credits him with a link (`credit:` in the front matter).
